5. Common pitfalls:

   * Backend stuck "not ready" → check vLLM reachability from inside the pod
     (kubectl exec ... -- curl ${VLLM_ENDPOINT%/v1}/v1/models).
   * RAG silently degraded → Triton offline. Retriever falls back to BM25 by
     design (see services/rag/retriever.py); this is logged but not fatal.
   * 429 on /api/chat → slowapi quota hit. Inspect RATE_LIMIT_STORAGE_URL.
